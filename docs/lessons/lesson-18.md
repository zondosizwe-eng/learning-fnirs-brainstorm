## Aim

Combine comparable participant results cautiously and produce a reproducible teaching package.

## Planned analysis decisions

1. Confirm a common anatomical space and montage interpretation.
2. Define the RC hand ROI consistently.
3. Choose a predefined response window rather than selecting each participant's most favourable time point.
4. Extract comparable HbO and HbR outcomes.
5. Review participant-level variability and trial counts.
6. Calculate group summaries suitable for the sample size and learning objective.
7. Clearly distinguish descriptive maps from inferential statistical results.

## Reporting package

- participant characteristics and recording summary;
- processing-parameter table;
- bad-channel and trial-exclusion table;
- individual HbO/HbR response figures;
- group-average time course with variability;
- cortical visualisations with explicit scale information;
- interpretation and limitations;
- student exercise and MCQs.

## Core caution

!!! warning
    A visually convincing group map is not sufficient evidence by itself. Group inference requires an appropriate participant-level outcome, consistent preprocessing and a statistical model that treats participants—not channels or time samples—as the independent observational units.
